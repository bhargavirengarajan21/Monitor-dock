🧠 Incident Memory

AI that remembers how your team fixed things. When an alert fires again, it recalls the past cause and the fix that worked — with citations — instead of letting someone rediscover it from scratch.
  1. 1
    Recall a fix On the Recall tab, pick a scenario and hit Fire alert. You'll see the facts first (matching incidents with similarity scores and the live error count), then the cited answer.
  2. 2
    Switch teams Use the team selector (top-right) to prove the permission filter: one team never sees another team's postmortem, even for the same error.
  3. 3
    Approve a postmortem On Review drafts, create a draft and Approve it — only then does it become searchable memory. Reject keeps it out.
  4. 4
    Honest misses Fire the Never-seen error scenario — the system says "No similar incident found" rather than guessing.
Everything runs offline — no keys or setup needed.
